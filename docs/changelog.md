- 11 Nov 2026: V4.2, Rob's changes (pull request, not live until Rob approves the side-by-side test in docs/research/2026-11-11_v4_2_side_by_side.md). (1) Results count only when the match finished FT: extra-time and penalties games are not used as form and are not graded (the stored score on those games was found to be the 120-minute score in some cases, e.g. single-leg FA Cup ties stored with a winner). One rule in config.FINISHED, used everywhere. (2) No averages in any prediction: the selection model is retrained on 38 counts (each side's last 5 league games and last 3 at the venue as counts, the competition's games with 3+ out of its last 50 this season); the per-game inputs and the division's goals per game are gone. (3) Trump card redefined as the whale rule: a side with 16+ goals in its last 5 league games carries the fixture to High unless the opponent kept 2+ clean sheets in its last 5; BTTS never assumed. The V4.1 both-teams version is retired (17 of 30 and 12 of 21 on the 14 leagues). (4) BTTS model removed (btts.py, btts_model.pkl, btts_rules.py, btts_audit.py, backtest_btts.py deleted, in git history); each card, the email and the review pack show each side's scoring counts instead; the BTTS audit step is out of ci/run.sh. (5) Per-game averages removed from report text (match-up lines, opposition line, xG line). (6) The performance report is counts only, split by model version, and leaves out extra-time and penalties results. Benchmarks rewritten for the V4.2 rules. Bookmaker prices stay out of selection and reports (Rob judges prices himself).
